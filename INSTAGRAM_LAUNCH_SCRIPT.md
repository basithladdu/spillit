# Spill It - Instagram Reel launch script

## Creative direction

**Length:** 27 seconds  
**Format:** 9:16 vertical, 1080 x 1920 export, 30 fps minimum  
**Mood:** intimate, curious, human; tactile details over startup spectacle

Keep every important word and logo inside the central safe area. Instagram's own guidance supports vertical Reels up to 9:16, with at least 30 fps and 720p; the safe-area rule keeps UI overlays from covering the message.

## Shot-by-shot edit

| Time | Picture | On-screen text | Audio / edit |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0-2s | Close-up of an empty chair, street corner, or bus-window reflection | **What happened here?** | Start with real ambient sound; cut on the first beat. |
| 2-6s | Fast screen recording: open Spill It and zoom toward a memory pin | **Every place carries a story.** | One clean map move; no cursor wandering. |
| 6-11s | Open the composer; show feeling, short caption, and location selection | **Pin the moment. Keep it anonymous.** | Match cuts to three taps. Never show private data. |
| 11-17s | Three tactile details: rain on glass, a cafe table, road lights at dusk | **The first kiss. The last goodbye. The tiny win.** | Let each phrase land on a separate detail. |
| 17-23s | Return to the published memory card and its map pin | **Something happened here. It mattered.** | Gentle push-in; hold long enough to read. |
| 23-27s | Spill It wordmark over a slow map pan | **Leave yours where it happened.** | Voiceover CTA, then a clean 0.5s end hold. |

## Voiceover

"We pass thousands of places without knowing what they mean to someone else. Spill It is a map of anonymous memories - the small moments, the secret ones, the stories that deserve a place to live. Pin yours. Let it stay."

## Caption

Some places are more than places.

Spill the memory you still carry - anonymously, right where it happened.

Your first kiss. A quiet goodbye. The day everything changed. The tiny moment nobody else saw.

What happened here?

#SpillIt #MemoryMap #AnonymousStories #DigitalStorytelling #MadeWithDevit

## Cover frame

Use the opening street detail with the short title **WHAT HAPPENED HERE?** Keep the title within the central safe area. Instagram's current help guidance lists 420 x 654 px as the recommended cover ratio; design the source cover larger, then crop-check it before posting.

## Filming and publishing checklist

- Record the product with seeded demo memories only; blur names, emails, exact private addresses, and account data.
- Keep captions high-contrast, large, and short. Do not place text at the very top or bottom where Instagram controls may overlap it.
- Export 1080 x 1920 at 30 fps or higher, with clear voice and low music under it.
- Use original ambient audio or music you have permission to publish. Avoid third-party stickers, GIFs, or copyrighted music if the Reel may be boosted.
- Add Instagram alt text: **A short video introducing Spill It, an anonymous map for sharing memories at the places where they happened.**
- Before publishing, watch once muted and once with sound. The story must still make sense both ways.
